# Smarter building mode

## Player outcome

The player can shape the campus instead of choosing from fixed decorative slots. Placement must feel predictable and mistakes must be recoverable.

## Rules

- Facilities use explicit rectangular footprints.
- Rotation advances in quarter turns and swaps width/depth where required.
- Placement and relocation reject overlaps with other facilities or protected world geometry.
- Moving an existing facility does not charge its purchase price again.
- Demolition removes exactly one selected facility and returns 40% of its original cost.
- Cash never becomes negative and a rejected action does not change the run.

## Acceptance examples

1. Rotating a non-square bench once swaps its footprint dimensions.
2. Placing a bench inside a restroom footprint is rejected.
3. Moving a facility preserves cash.
4. Demolishing a €16.00 restroom returns €6.40.

## Non-goals

- Free-form terrain editing.
- Multi-select or blueprint copying.
- Construction timers.
