# Business and service animations

## Purpose

Give expansion businesses a visible operating identity instead of relying on signage and projected income alone.

## Activity contract

- Gate Bakery exposes a glowing oven, pastry trays, preparation, and serving.
- Village Gym and the Sports Fitness Centre operate treadmills, weights, and exercise activity.
- Padel courts show booked players, a moving ball, and a visible rally.
- Village Laundry rotates individual machine drums and shows collection baskets and staff activity.
- The seafood restaurant fills tables and shows tray service and clearing movement.
- Surf rental visibly removes and returns boards from its rack.
- Parking shows occupied bays and arriving or departing vehicles.
- The conference centre runs a presentation screen with seated delegates and a live pointer.

Animations respond to real capacity, breakdowns, active guests, queues, and campaign time. Broken or depleted businesses stop operating.

All scenes share a grounded miniature art system: consistent character scale, equipment shadows, layered materials, restrained motion trails, and props aligned to the isometric venue footprint.

## Acceptance checks

Automated coverage verifies business-to-animation mapping, operating gates, guest-driven intensity, and renderer integration. Manual review checks that activity remains inside each business footprint and stays readable at normal camera zoom.
