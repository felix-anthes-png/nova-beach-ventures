# Campus calendar and guest groups

## Purpose

The campus should not generate the same crowd every day. A recurring calendar gives the player a forecast, changes the visitor mix, and makes pricing, stock, hours, staffing, facilities, and events respond to a recognisable business situation.

## Calendar

The campaign uses a persistent seven-day programme:

1. Lecture Day
2. Exam Day
3. Career Fair
4. Campus Open Day
5. Surf Tournament
6. Festival Evening
7. Coastal Weekend

Each day defines an attendance multiplier, phase-specific footfall, likely guest groups, a description, and a preparation focus. The programme repeats in week two without resetting the campaign.

## Guest groups

The live crowd contains students, professors, campus staff, tourists, surfers, corporate guests, local residents, and families. Every group has its own:

- budget range;
- queue patience;
- price tolerance;
- venue preferences;
- preferred attraction intensity;
- schedule across the six daily phases;
- colour and visible accessory.

Individual variation remains deterministic inside each group.

## Reporting

- The schedule panel shows today, the next seven days, expected attendance, crowd composition, and the preparation focus.
- Guest intelligence shows the live group mix, purchases, and spending.
- Individual profiles explain group identity and price tolerance.
- Closing reports and ledger history preserve the calendar day and results by guest group.

## Acceptance checks

1. The seven day types repeat in the same order and survive reopening.
2. Festival Evening forecasts more guests than a normal Lecture Day with the same campus.
3. A Surf Tournament produces a surfer-led crowd while Lecture Day remains student-led.
4. Guest groups have visibly different budgets, patience, preferences, price tolerance, and schedules.
5. Higher price tolerance improves the perceived appeal of the same valid offer without changing the fixed 10/5/0 demand rule.
6. Daily history records the calendar type and guest-group performance exactly once.
7. Existing campaigns migrate to the new calendar without losing balances or progression.
