# Campus circulation and guest agendas

## Purpose

Make the expanded campus behave as one connected place. New districts should create journeys through the original Academic Core instead of retaining every visitor beside their arrival plot.

## Player-visible behaviour

- Most guests enter through the main campus entrance.
- Commuters use the Transit arrival once that district is owned, then cross the campus for their scheduled activity.
- A limited share of surfers and tourists may enter directly from the beach.
- Guest agendas respond to arrival, lecture, lunch, afternoon, sunset, and evening phases.
- Morning, lecture, lunch, afternoon, and sunset agendas deliberately use the Academic Core before free commercial decisions.
- Trips between the original campus and the eastern districts follow a shared pedestrian spine.
- Developed campuses can display up to 72 guests without changing the underlying economic visitor total.

## Acceptance checks

1. A commuter arriving through Transit receives an agenda containing a central-campus stop.
2. An eastern guest walking to the Academic Core crosses the pedestrian spine from east to west.
3. A core guest walking to an eastern district crosses the same spine from west to east.
4. Direct beach arrival is an exception, not the default for every tourist and surfer.
5. Agenda stops are completed before normal early-exit rules may send a guest home.
6. The existing needs, queue, pricing, sales, and persistence systems remain unchanged.

## Out of scope

This batch does not physically move district parcels or buildings. A later map-composition batch can place districts on both sides once the traffic model has been validated.
