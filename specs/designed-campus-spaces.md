# Designed campus spaces

## Purpose

The campus should read as a planned place, not as a set of unrelated buildings on an open tile field. This batch gives the original coast district a clear spatial hierarchy while preserving the existing economy, construction catalogue, progression, and save format.

## Player-facing contract

- The coast district contains eight visually distinct places: Academic Courtyard, Central Arrival Plaza, Shaded Café Terrace, Food Court, Student Lawn, Beach Promenade, Event Square, and Service & Delivery Lane.
- Each place has its own paving or landscape material, edge treatment, furniture, and small ground-level name marker.
- The spaces share the existing Atlantic-modernist palette so they still belong to the same campus.
- Critical entrances and circulation routes cannot be blocked by construction. Social spaces remain available for compatible player-built facilities.
- Guest routes pass through the arrival plaza, academic courtyard, food court, lawn, promenade, or service lane according to their destination instead of crossing the map on generic horizontal lanes.
- Existing saves remain compatible and the business simulation is unchanged.

## Acceptance checks

1. All eight spaces have dedicated renderers and are present in the world composition.
2. A guest entering from the west uses the Arrival Plaza before continuing to a destination.
3. Academic visitors route through the Academic Courtyard.
4. Beach and event visitors use the Beach Promenade.
5. Food-court and lawn destinations create visible crowd flow through their respective public spaces.
6. The Arrival Plaza, Academic Courtyard, and Service Lane reject conflicting construction.
7. Syntax checks and the complete automated suite pass.
