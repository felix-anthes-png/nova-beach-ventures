# Managed DJ operations

## Player outcome

The DJ deck is a business operation rather than a passive scenery item. The player schedules and configures an event, accepts its costs, and watches the resulting crowd gather.

## Configuration

- Afternoon or sunset time slot
- Music style
- Positive ticket price
- Hired DJ
- Speaker, lighting, shade, and capacity upgrades

## Forecast

Before scheduling, show expected attendance, revenue, operating cost, profit, excitement, noise, comfort, and capacity.

## Rules

- A built DJ deck and hired DJ are required.
- Scheduling reserves operating cost once.
- Ticket price and music style materially affect attendance.
- Upgrades alter the forecast and visible venue profile.
- Guests cannot select a ticket they cannot afford.
- The event starts at its scheduled time, records one result, and ends once.

## Non-goals

- Licensed music playback.
- Simultaneous events at multiple DJ decks.
- Unannounced random cancellation.
