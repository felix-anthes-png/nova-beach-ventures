# Environmental motion

## Intent

The outdoor campus should feel exposed to one Atlantic climate rather than like a collection of static props. Motion must reinforce the existing architectural model without distracting from queues, construction, or management overlays.

## Behaviour

- The ocean uses layered travelling swells, shoreline foam, and small drifting foam patches.
- Clouds and distant birds move across the sky at speeds derived from the same environmental profile.
- Palms, campus flags, promenade pennants, and terrace umbrellas respond to the current breeze.
- Courtyard water jets and ripples animate continuously.
- The breeze varies predictably with campaign day and time, and the weather chip reports calm surf, Atlantic breeze, or coastal wind.
- Time-of-day haze remains connected to the existing morning, sunset, and dusk lighting stages.
- Reduced-motion preferences keep the composition and environmental state while freezing non-essential movement.

## Acceptance checks

1. The outdoor map visibly moves when campaign time is paused; simulation finances remain unchanged.
2. Ocean, vegetation, flags, and sky share the same wind intensity instead of using unrelated animation speeds.
3. Entering an interior removes outdoor sky and coastal effects.
4. The weather chip reflects the currently rendered environmental profile.
5. With reduced motion enabled, the environment remains readable without continuous drifting or swaying.
