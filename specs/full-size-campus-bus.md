# Full-size campus bus and grounded district buildings

## Purpose

Replace the symbolic Transit bus with a believable vehicle arrival and correct the weak ground contact of enclosed expansion buildings.

## Bus cycle

- A full-size Nova campus bus approaches along the Transit arrival lane every 120 in-game minutes.
- It brakes at the authored bus stop, opens a full-height passenger door, and waits.
- Up to ten full-size passengers leave in staggered waves and spread onto the pavement.
- The door closes before the bus departs along the same lane.
- The vehicle has windows, route display, body panels, stripe, wheels, lights, and a real collision footprint.
- The bus only operates when the Transit district and bus business are available and not broken.

## Building grounding

Every enclosed district venue receives a seven-unit foundation plinth, stronger contact shadow, darker base faces, and a front edge highlight. The building and foundation remain aligned to the existing business footprint.

## Acceptance checks

Automated coverage verifies all bus phases, passenger release, stop position, full-size rendering, vehicle collision, and foundation integration. Manual review checks depth order, lane alignment, door placement, and pedestrian scale.
