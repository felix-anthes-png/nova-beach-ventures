# Lecture hall attendance

## Purpose

Make the Academic Wing function as a university building by giving scheduled students and professors a real lecture destination.

## Behaviour

- The Academic Wing contains a tiered lecture hall with three seating rows, a presentation screen and a lectern.
- Morning-arrival and lecture-block agendas that include the Academic Wing route guests through its real entrance.
- The same live guest crosses into the interior, walks down the side aisle, takes a seat and remains for a timed lecture.
- Professors use a presentation pose while students sit and take notes.
- Completing the lecture records one campus activity, advances the guest agenda and sends the guest back through the entrance to the Academic Courtyard.
- Lunch, afternoon, sunset and leisure agendas do not invent lecture attendance when the Academic Wing is not part of the timetable route.

## Acceptance checks

1. A student with an Academic Wing agenda enters the main building instead of stopping outside.
2. The interior route avoids the café counter, furniture and lecture seating footprints.
3. Students visibly sit and study while professors visibly present.
4. Lecture attendance lasts longer than a normal retail interaction.
5. A lecture creates one recorded activity and no commercial transaction.
6. After class, the guest exits to the Academic Courtyard and continues its campus agenda.
