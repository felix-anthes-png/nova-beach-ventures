# Tycoon progression

## Player outcome

The campus begins small and becomes visibly more valuable as the business succeeds. Cash and company experience should unlock meaningful choices rather than reveal an already-built park.

## Rules

- A new campaign starts at level one with Atlantic Café only.
- Fuel Bar, Merch Lab, Beach Bar, and Surf Shack unlock through the level roadmap.
- Stores have three visible operating levels.
- Staff roles unlock progressively and affect queues, cleanliness, repair, hosting, or entertainment.
- East Promenade must be acquired before the Beach Bar.
- Ocean Pier follows later and expands the playable/buildable map.
- The Coastline Pavilion supports timed crowd events.
- Long-term goals include at least 250 visitors and a 90% campus rating.

## Acceptance examples

1. A new run contains only Atlantic Café and the Coast district.
2. Fuel Bar and service staff cannot be bought before level two.
3. Beach Bar cannot be bought before East Promenade.
4. A launched event increases the live guest count and ends exactly once.
5. Visitor and rating goals complete when their measured thresholds are reached.
