# Living campus time and logistics

## Player outcome

The campus follows a recognisable daily rhythm. The player plans opening hours, stock orders, and events around predictable waves rather than simulating an entire day with one button.

## Daily timetable

| Period | Time | Footfall character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morning arrival | 07:30–09:30 | Strong incoming crowd |
| Morning lectures | 09:30–11:30 | Quiet paths and lower demand |
| Lunch rush | 11:30–14:00 | Highest food-and-drink demand |
| Afternoon lectures | 14:00–16:30 | Reduced general footfall |
| Afternoon break | 16:30–17:30 | Short secondary rush |
| Sunset period | 17:30–19:30 | Event-oriented coastal crowd |

## Store hours

- Every unlocked store has an opening and closing time.
- A trading window must last at least two hours and remain inside the campus day.
- Closed stores make no sales and are excluded from guest destinations.

## Deliveries

- Orders reserve their full purchase cost immediately.
- Deliveries arrive at 08:15, 12:15, or 16:15.
- Orders placed after a window's cutoff use the next available window.
- A late final-window order rolls to the following morning.
- Stock changes only when the delivery is processed.
- A delivery is recorded once and cannot replay after reload.

## Day close

- At 19:30 the completed day is appended to history once.
- The report includes revenue, cost of goods sold, profit, units, visitors, and deliveries.
- The next day begins at 07:30 without ending or pausing the campaign.
- The report is informational and can remain visible while time continues.
