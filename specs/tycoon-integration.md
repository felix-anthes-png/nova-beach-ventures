# Tycoon integration and balancing

## Player experience

Campus growth now creates a connected operating loop. Better facilities, service, stock availability, and financial performance build attendance momentum. Larger crowds increase sales opportunities while creating more queue, cleanliness, maintenance, staffing, and overhead pressure.

The Tycoon Control Room explains the current management score, expected attendance, daily fixed costs, immediate priorities, and the factors that changed momentum at the last day close.

## Rules

- Every open store has a daily operating cost based on its level and opening hours.
- Acquired expansion districts add daily upkeep.
- Store upgrades improve visible queue service speed but increase overhead and equipment wear.
- Daily operating costs are charged once at 19:30 and included in operating profit.
- Unaffordable operating costs become overdue without making cash negative.
- Attendance momentum is saved and updates once per completed day.
- Rating, operating profit, product availability, unresolved work orders, and queue walkaways influence momentum.
- Momentum changes expected guest volume but does not change the original 10 / 5 / 0 product demand boundaries.

## Acceptance checks

1. A new company starts at 50 momentum with a visible Atlantic Café operating cost.
2. Upgrading a store increases service speed and operating cost; shorter opening hours reduce its cost.
3. A completed day records operating costs and reconciles operating profit after COGS, events, wages, and overhead.
4. A poorly stocked loss-making day reduces momentum; a stocked profitable day increases it.
5. Overdue campus bills never make cash negative and can be settled later.
6. The control room identifies stock, pricing, maintenance, payroll, and campus-quality pressure.
7. Existing saves migrate once and retain their business state.

## Non-goals

- Loans, interest, taxation, or bankruptcy.
- Random economic events.
- New stores or districts in this batch.
