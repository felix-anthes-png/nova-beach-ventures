# Visual world redesign

## Goal

Make the campus read as one designed place rather than a collection of isolated props, while making shops and people easier to recognise during play.

## Player-facing scope

- Campus buildings share Atlantic-modernist concrete, teal glass, timber, planted roofs, and coral or yellow wayfinding accents.
- Colonnades, paving seams, planters, and signage connect major buildings into courtyards and circulation routes.
- Atlantic Café, Fuel Bar, Merch Lab, Maré Beach Bar, and Surf Shack each use a different architectural type.
- Venue levels remain visible through added terraces, planting, displays, shade, or roof treatment.
- Indoor rooms keep the same exterior material language but use distinct layouts and floor zoning.
- Guests and the player use stable foot anchors, restrained walk cycles, contact shadows, varied hair, and clearer silhouettes.

## Acceptance checks

1. Each unlocked store is recognisable from its building shape without relying on its label.
2. Store level two and three add visible architectural detail without moving the interaction point.
3. Walking guests keep both feet visually attached to the ground plane and do not flip or jump when direction changes.
4. Academic, Mercado, and Hub interiors retain their own identity while matching the outdoor palette.
5. Existing saves load without migration and all economy, progression, and operations tests remain unchanged.
